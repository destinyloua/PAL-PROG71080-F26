# Midterm Practice Project
# Version 1

## Scenario:
The mayor of Bikini Bottom has asked you to perform some basic data analysis on some of the residents. Please answer the mayor's questions about Bikini Bottom's residents: 

1. Who is the richest resident in Bikini Bottom? Provide their name and cash amount. 
2. Which residents are restaurant owners? Provide their names and their jobs. 
3. Which residents have been flagged as unfriendly? Provide their name, job and friendly status (false). 

Additionally, if you are interested in working with the Bikini Bottom government further, they have provided extra challenges for you to complete. These challenges are optional, but they may be worth trying and may potentially help you with your future endeavors: 

1. Create a function that rank residents from lowest to highest cash amount. Display the ranked list with the resident's name and their cash amount. 
2. Create a function that counts how many residents work for each job type. Display the job and the number of residents working for the job.
